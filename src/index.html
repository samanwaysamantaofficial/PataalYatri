Pataal Yatriপাতাল যাত্রী

One way down, and no clean version

Pataal Yatri (পাতাল যাত্রী) is Bengali for the one who travels the underworld. The name is the method.

The work starts where the lamps of a Bengali evening stop reaching: the burning ground, the blood-red hibiscus, the stories told to keep children indoors after dark. None of it is softened, translated or tidied. If a take sounds unhinged, that is the take.

Underneath it is an older idea: every civilization is built on top of the one it buried. The records dig.
